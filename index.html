Upload a surface image and select Analyze surface. This prototype classifies crack presence and shows a provisional surface-condition indicator; it does not measure crack severity or determine structural safety.

Surface repair / plaster

May be suitable only when an inspection confirms the crack is superficial and limited to the plaster or finish.

Structural repair / rebuilding

Cannot be decided from this image classifier. A qualified structural engineer must assess the wall and determine the appropriate action.

Safety note: if cracks are wide, growing, diagonal/stair-step, accompanied by bulging or movement, or the wall appears unstable, keep people away from the area and seek urgent professional assessment.
